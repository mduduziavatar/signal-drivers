Frequently asked questions

What equipment do I need to join?

Any bi-directional-capable scanner (XTOOL, Autel, Launch and similar are all supported). No scanner yet? See the equipment financing option above.

How does equipment financing work?

Signal fronts the cost of a scanner against your future jobs. You run at a 30% commission instead of 20% until it's paid off, then drop to the standard rate — no upfront cost to start earning.

How do I get matched to jobs?

Jobs are filtered to your registered equipment's capabilities and your location, so you only see work you can actually do.

When do I get paid?

Payment is collected through the platform and your 80% split pays out automatically once the job is marked complete — no invoicing, no chasing customers.

How am I vetted?

A one-time verification before you can accept jobs, then your rating and completed-job history build your reputation on the network from there.

Can I set my own prices?

Pricing bands are set by local market rates at launch. If a job needs work outside the listed scope once you're on site, you quote that separately before doing it.
